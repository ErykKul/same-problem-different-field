MECHANISM: A pulsar population is synthesized using the PsrPopPy Python module, sampling from statistical distributions of period, luminosity, dispersion measure, spectral index, and duty cycle. For simulated pulsars, three detection methods are modeled: (1) image-domain SNR via the radiometer equation, (2) time-domain periodic SNR after de-dispersion accounting for scattering and smearing, and (3) scintillation visibility correlation search (SVCS) SNR via cross-correlation of dynamic spectra. Detection efficiencies are computed for different observational parameters. Pulsars detected by each method are identified and compared to find unique detections.
