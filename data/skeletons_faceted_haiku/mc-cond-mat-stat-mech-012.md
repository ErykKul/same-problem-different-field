MECHANISM: This paper studies phase diagram and critical behavior of a non-Hermitian Ising-Gamma spin chain with dissipative loss/gain terms by mapping to fermionic representation via Jordan-Wigner transformation. The mechanism involves exact diagonalization through Bogoliubov transformation to obtain quasiparticle energies with complex components. Ground state properties and order parameters are computed using Pfaffian formulas for multi-point correlation functions. Phase boundaries are identified via energy gap closure and discontinuity in second derivative of ground-state energy. Critical exponents are extracted from finite-size scaling of entanglement entropy. Spiral phases with tunable chirality emerge from competing Ising and Gamma interactions, with dissipation expanding spiralphase region.
