MECHANISM: A historical and epistemological analysis of the dark matter hypothesis versus modified gravity as explanations for anomalies in celestial motion. The paper traces how Newton's theory explained planetary orbits through gravitational force, how Einstein's general relativity resolved the Mercurian perihelion anomaly, and how the missing mass problem at galactic scales emerged in the 1930s-1970s. It discusses whether dark matter is an invisible component (like Neptune) or evidence of fundamental theory breakdown (like Mercury suggesting general relativity over Newton).
