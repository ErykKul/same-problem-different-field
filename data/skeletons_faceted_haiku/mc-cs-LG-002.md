MECHANISM: A theoretical analysis of reinforcement learning with linear function approximation under non-Markov state and cost processes. Policy evaluation convergence is established under ergodicity conditions via a joint operator composed of orthogonal projection and an auxiliary Markov decision process Bellman operator. For Q-learning, convergence is shown for basis functions derived from quantization maps. Error bounds are derived for partially observed Markov decision processes using finite-memory state representations.
