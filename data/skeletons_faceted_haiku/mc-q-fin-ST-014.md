MECHANISM: The paper reviews and unifies theoretical models of asset price volatility across multiple scales, including rough volatility models, multifractal models, and their connections. Rough volatility assumes log-volatility follows a fractional Brownian motion with Hurst exponent H ≈ 0.1, leading to stochastic Volterra equations. Multifractal models employ discrete cascades with logarithmic covariance decay. Logarithmic Stationary fractional Brownian Motion (Log S-fBM) provides a unifying framework interpolating between rough volatility (finite H) and multifractal asymptotics (H → 0+). The analysis derives implications for option pricing, implied volatility skew, and model calibration across different time horizons, connecting financial modeling with extreme value theory and fractional processes.
