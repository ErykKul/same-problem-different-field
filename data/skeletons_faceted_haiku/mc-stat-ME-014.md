The modified criterion BIC_HES equals classical BIC plus the log-determinant of the Fisher information. Derive this modification from second-order Taylor expansion of the log-likelihood and the Laplace approximation to the marginal likelihood. The Fisher information determinant encodes the local information content and hierarchical structure, providing adaptive penalization especially effective for small samples or mixed-effects models.
