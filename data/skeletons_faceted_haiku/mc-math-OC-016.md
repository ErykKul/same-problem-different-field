MECHANISM: The paper analyzes pricing and hedging of European options in financial markets with multiple default events using backward stochastic differential equations (BSDEs). The model incorporates default times as stopping times and uses BSDE theory to derive recursive equations for option value. Solution techniques solve the coupled forward-backward system to obtain option prices and hedging strategies under default risk.
