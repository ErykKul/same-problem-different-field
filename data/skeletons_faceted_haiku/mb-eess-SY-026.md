MECHANISM: The network dynamics are modeled as a continuous-time Markov chain where agents exchange state information via gossip, accepting packets based on timestamp comparison. Two accuracy metrics are computed via stochastic hybrid systems analysis: average accuracy (expected fraction of agents holding correct state in a subset) and freshness-based accuracy (correctness of the newest packet in a subset). Steady-state balance equations and matrix-valued recursions characterize these metrics under different source evolution models, and the contribution of direct versus gossip-mediated updates is quantified.
