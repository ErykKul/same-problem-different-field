MECHANISM: The paper evaluates journal impact metrics within the Scilit bibliographic database. It compares the Integrated Impact Indicator (I3) and I3/N metrics against Journal Impact Factor (JIF) and CiteScore by acquiring publication and citation metadata from multiple authoritative sources (Crossref, PubMed, DOAJ), performing data matching, disambiguation, and normalization across 17,816 journals. The I3 metric assigns weights to publications based on citation percentile ranks (top 1%, 10%, 50%, bottom 50%), sums weighted contributions, and normalizes by publication count (I3/N). Field normalization uses fractional citation counting to adjust for reference-list length differences. Statistical analyses include descriptive statistics and distributional comparisons.
