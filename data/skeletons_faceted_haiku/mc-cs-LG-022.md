MECHANISM: A survey examining the connections between neuromorphic computing models, state-space models, and Transformer architectures distinguishes between intra-token processing (transformations within feature vectors) and inter-token processing (contextual combination across sequence elements). The paper reviews spiking neural networks for intra-token operations and sparse attention mechanisms for inter-token operations, categorizing how modern architectures leverage neuromorphic principles through quantized activations and sparse dynamics. Training methodologies including surrogate gradients and reinforcement-learning-based local rules are discussed.
