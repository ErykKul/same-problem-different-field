MECHANISM: Model linguistic convergence and divergence in multiple populations via the replicator-mutator equation. Each population adopts one of two competing linguistic variants; fitness is extrinsic, depending on ingroup convergence strength and outgroup divergence strength. Variant frequencies evolve according to replicator-mutator dynamics, which incorporate both selection (via frequency-dependent payoffs) and mutation (stochastic innovation/error).
