MECHANISM: Acquires single-channel EEG time series and estimates the power spectral density via periodogram. Develops a statistical model for the noise spectrum by fitting a mixture of sinusoids and a polynomial background. Uses the model to characterize frequency-domain properties of brain-computer interface signals in the steady-state visual evoked potential regime. Estimates unknown parameters of the mixture model via frequency-domain optimization.
