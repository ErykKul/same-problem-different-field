MECHANISM: This paper derives spin-operator matrix elements (form factors) for the critical Ising chain directly at the critical point using fermionic basis states. The mechanism involves diagonalizing the Hamiltonian via Bogoliubov transformation on Majorana fermions to obtain a free fermion spectrum with momentum-dependent energies. Form factors are computed between eigenstates in different charge sectors (R and NS sectors) using finite-volume Fermi mode decomposition. Square-root products of roots of unity simplify leg functions, which reduce to classical integrals (Binet's second integral) in the scaling limit. Convergence to Ising CFT is established via Fredholm determinant identities for two-point correlators on a cylinder.
