MECHANISM: Online reinforcement learning algorithms with sublinear regret bounds are reformulated as history-dependent policies in non-rectangular robust MDP setting under average-reward criterion, shown to be robust-optimal. An epoch-based policy is constructed that alternates between executing a candidate stationary policy and running a Markov chain sequential probability ratio test (SPRT) with anytime-valid error control and logarithmic detection delay. Upon test rejection or epoch expiration, switches to online RL routine. Policy structure is analyzed through transient value bounds relating finite-time performance to regret rates. The framework establishes minimax representation of robust value through infimum over ambiguity set of classical optimal gains.
