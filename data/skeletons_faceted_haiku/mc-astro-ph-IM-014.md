MECHANISM: A magnetohydrodynamic simulation of a Milky-Way-like galaxy ISM patch is run over 100 Myr. At each of 11 time snapshots, turbulent density and velocity fluctuations are isolated from non-turbulent contributions by Gaussian-kernel smoothing at 100 pc scales. Turbulence-isolated density dispersion, sonic Mach number, and magnetic plasma beta are computed for warm neutral medium gas. The turbulence driving parameter is calculated via a closed-form relation connecting these quantities. Time series analysis and Spearman correlation analysis relate the driving parameter evolution to star formation rates and supernova feedback delays.
