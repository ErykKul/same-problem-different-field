MECHANISM: A design probe conducting qualitative interviews and thematic analysis with 20 musicians to understand current Digital Audio Workstation workflows and speculate on future Mixed Reality collaboration scenarios. The study comprises three stages: interviews on current DAW usage, hands-on evaluation of a prototype MR system with networked loop pedal control, and reflective interviews asking participants to envision future collaborative composition in MR environments.
