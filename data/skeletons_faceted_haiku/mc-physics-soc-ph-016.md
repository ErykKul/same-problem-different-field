MECHANISM: A susceptible-infected-susceptible disease model on hypergraphs incorporates two transmission modes: dyadic transmission via pairwise edges (direct contacts) and polyadic transmission via hyperedges of size three or larger (environmental or group spread). Each hyperedge has an associated recovery rate; node-level and hyperedge-level state variables track infection status. Mean-field approximations derive threshold conditions for disease persistence on uniform and regular hypergraphs. Numerical simulations examine disease dynamics under varying hyperedge-size distributions and recovery rates, comparing effects across transmission pathways.
DOMAIN: Epidemic modeling, higher-order networks, disease transmission
STRUCTURE: Finite-state machine
DATA_OBJECT: Graph or network
INFERENCE: Deterministic or closed-form
PROBLEM_FORM: Proof or characterization
DISTRIBUTION: binary; binary
COMPLEXITY: closed-form; polynomial iterative; finite-sample bound
