MECHANISM: Stochastic gradient descent iteratively updates a parameter estimate by taking steps along noisy gradients computed on individual data points. Under temporal dependence in covariates and noise, convergence rates and regret bounds are established. The final iterate is analyzed for its estimation accuracy and inference properties. SGD automatically accommodates both independent and dependent information under broad stepsize schedules.
DOMAIN: Stochastic optimization, statistical learning, time series
STRUCTURE: optimization only
DATA_OBJECT: sequence or time-series
INFERENCE: frequentist point estimate
PROBLEM_FORM: estimation
DISTRIBUTION: none
COMPLEXITY: convergence rate
