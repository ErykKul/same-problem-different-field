MECHANISM: An empirical accessibility and usability evaluation of 212 Bangladeshi government and non-government websites using automated accessibility assessment tools against WCAG 2.2 standards, complemented by user survey data from 103 participants on real-world accessibility, usability, and security experiences. Comparative analysis identifies disparities between website categories and persistent barriers in navigation, readability, and feature adoption.
