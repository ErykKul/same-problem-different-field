MECHANISM: An empirical study investigates the relationship between information overload metrics and fake news proliferation on social networks by extracting topic distributions using a neural topic model and computing statistical correlations with fake news labels detected via a separate neural classifier. The analysis quantifies global and community-level associations between derived overload metrics and misinformation prevalence.
DOMAIN: information quality, social media analysis
STRUCTURE: other: correlation analysis of text distributions
DATA_OBJECT: sequence or time-series
INFERENCE: frequentist point estimate
PROBLEM_FORM: estimation
DISTRIBUTION: continuous, normal
COMPLEXITY: polynomial iterative
