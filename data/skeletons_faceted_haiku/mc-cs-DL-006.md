MECHANISM: L-PRISMA extends PRISMA (Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic Reviews) guidelines to incorporate generative AI while maintaining reproducibility and auditability. The framework combines human-led synthesis with a GenAI-assisted statistical pre-screening step, where large language models support data extraction and literature screening. A deterministic statistical layer is introduced to enhance reproducibility despite the non-deterministic nature of LLMs. The paper is primarily methodological guidance for systematic review processes using AI tools, not a novel computational algorithm.
