MECHANISM: Converts tiny gravitational (Shapiro) time delays from spacetime curvature into measurable optical phase shifts using a fiber-optic Sagnac interferometer. Two fiber loops (each ~100 km) at different heights sample a differential propagation delay proportional to the parametrized post-Newtonian parameter γ. Light circulates, accumulating phase difference related to delay difference. Phase bias is set to quadrature for maximum sensitivity; signal encoding via intensity modulation at GHz frequencies; demodulation extracts low-frequency delay signal. Noise sources (RIN, shot, thermal) are characterized; post-processing via oversampling and digital filtering improves signal-to-noise.
