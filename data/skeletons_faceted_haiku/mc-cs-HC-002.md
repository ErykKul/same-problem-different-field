MECHANISM: An experimental study comparing three system configurations (unrestricted holographic setup, AR glasses with limited 52-degree field of view, and AR glasses with mini-avatar notifications) by measuring reaction time to avatar gestures, co-presence perception, and enjoyment. Participants complete a hand-drumming task while identifying which avatar is looking at them across three within-subjects conditions, with quantitative measures and qualitative feedback collected.
