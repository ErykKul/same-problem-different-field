MECHANISM: Dictionary learning is formulated as a matrix factorization problem: recover a dictionary from training signals where each signal is a sparse linear combination. The problem minimizes total L1-norm of coefficients subject to exact representation constraint. Local minima are characterized algebraically via subdifferential conditions on a nonconvex L1 criterion. For orthonormal bases and incoherent dictionaries, conditions ensure a dictionary is a local minimum. A probabilistic analysis shows that under Bernoulli-Gaussian sparsity, sufficiently incoherent bases are locally identifiable with N approximately CK log K samples.
DOMAIN: Signal processing - sparse representations and dictionary learning
STRUCTURE: sparse linear algebra
DATA_OBJECT: dense matrix or tensor
INFERENCE: optimization only
PROBLEM_FORM: estimation
DISTRIBUTION: none
COMPLEXITY: polynomial iterative
