MECHANISM: A convex optimization framework reconstructs images from incomplete Fourier measurements via L1-norm minimization under sparsity or compressibility constraints. The approach replaces standard matching pursuit (CLEAN algorithm) with convex minimization, allowing incorporation of prior information. The objective minimizes L1-norm of sparse representation subject to consistency with observed Fourier data. The convex relaxation enables global optimization and robustness. Application addresses both point source reconstruction and diffuse field recovery.
DOMAIN: Radio astronomy - interferometric image reconstruction
STRUCTURE: sparse linear algebra
DATA_OBJECT: dense matrix or tensor
INFERENCE: optimization only
PROBLEM_FORM: reconstruction or denoising
DISTRIBUTION: none
COMPLEXITY: polynomial iterative
