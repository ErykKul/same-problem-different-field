MECHANISM: Philosophical analysis of grounding and scientific explanation without computational method. The paper develops a conceptual framework (not an algorithm) for understanding how scientific explanation and ontology relate to relational invariance and stabilisation rather than metaphysical grounding. The paper argues that grounding regresses at foundational levels (space, time, quantum measurement) and proposes adequacy as a criterion for explanation instead. Applications are conceptual case studies rather than computational procedures.
