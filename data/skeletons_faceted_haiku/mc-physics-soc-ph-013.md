MECHANISM: A discrete-state Markov process formulation of epidemic dynamics on networks uses indicator functions to track infection states. The analysis derives a hierarchy of evolution equations for marginal probabilities, analogous to the BBGKY hierarchy in statistical mechanics. Closure approximations are needed to truncate the infinite hierarchy; simplified closures are validated via Monte Carlo simulations. The framework clarifies challenges of capturing stochasticity and network topology effects on infection propagation and is applied to COVID-19 spread with lockdown interventions.
DOMAIN: Network epidemiology, stochastic processes, BBGKY closure
STRUCTURE: Finite-state machine
DATA_OBJECT: Graph or network
INFERENCE: Sampling or Monte-Carlo
PROBLEM_FORM: Simulation or generation
