MECHANISM: High-resolution solar observations (IBIS) of a magnetic pore are progressively degraded in angular and detector resolution by convolving with synthetic Airy disk PSFs and binning pixels. For each degradation level, line-of-sight Doppler velocity time series are extracted within magnetic field regions. Power spectral density is computed via periodogram for each pixel, then averaged. The analysis examines how spectral power in the 3 mHz and 5 mHz frequency bands shifts as resolution degrades. Degraded data are resampled to simulate observations from another instrument (HMI) for comparison.
