MECHANISM: A stochastic model of entity size dynamics combines proportional growth (geometric Brownian motion) with random birth and death processes to derive the steady-state size distribution. Entities arrive as a random point process with time-varying rate; each entity's size grows according to a continuous stochastic process where the volatility scales proportionally to current size; entities exit when size falls below a threshold or when an exogenous shock occurs; a balance condition requiring that the sum of mean growth rate, birth rate, and death rate vanish ensures the steady-state density follows a power law with tail exponent one. Deviations from this balance condition produce modified power laws with different tail exponents; extensions allow growth rate and volatility to deviate from exact proportionality while preserving the power law for large sizes.
