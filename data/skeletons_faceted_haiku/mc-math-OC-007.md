MECHANISM: The paper studies a coupled nonlinear system containing a parabolic PDE, a quasivariational inequality, and a parabolic variational inequality in Banach spaces. The system is solved using a fixed-point iteration approach that decomposes the problem into sub-problems: first finding a solution to the variational inequality for a given field value, then solving the parabolic PDE with a nonlinear source term derived from the inequality solution. The fixed point theorem guarantees unique solvability under Lipschitz and monotonicity conditions on the operators. The method estimates solutions using energy techniques and applies to a viscoelastic contact problem with friction, wear, and damage.
