MECHANISM: Collects point-level measurements of multiple trace metals and derives aggregate pollution indices (SPI, Nemerow, modified contamination degree, ecological risk). Fits spatial interpolation models including ordinary kriging, ordinary cokriging, and artificial neural networks to the measured values. Uses semivariogram analysis to characterize spatial autocorrelation of each element. Compares model predictions via cross-validation using root mean square error, coefficient of determination, scatter index, and bias. Selects best model and produces spatial prediction maps for unmeasured locations.
DOMAIN: Environmental geostatistics, trace metal assessment, mining site remediation
STRUCTURE: other: kriging and cokriging
DATA_OBJECT: point set
INFERENCE: frequentist point estimate
PROBLEM_FORM: prediction
DISTRIBUTION: continuous; continuous
COMPLEXITY: polynomial iterative
