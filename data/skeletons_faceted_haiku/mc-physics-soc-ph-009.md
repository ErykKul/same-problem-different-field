MECHANISM: The model represents degree fluctuations in fixed-size networks as a state-dependent random walk process with position-dependent diffusion coefficients. Each entity's degree evolves as a stochastic process without net node growth or preferential attachment. Theoretical analysis proves convergence to power-law degree distributions under mild conditions on absorbing states. Network resilience is assessed by analyzing the size of the largest connected component and mean shortest paths after targeted degree-based attacks, validated against real-world network data.
