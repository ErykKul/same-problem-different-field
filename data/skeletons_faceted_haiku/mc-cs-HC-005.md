MECHANISM: A feasibility study conducting a single-session intervention where 100 employees complete a cognitive reappraisal activity mediated by a GPT-4o chatbot. Pre-post measures of perceived stress intensity, stress mindset, perceived demand, and perceived resources are collected via Likert scales. Sentiment and stress progression across conversation quartiles are analyzed using RoBERTa classifiers and LLM-based stress ratings. Qualitative themes are extracted from open-ended responses via thematic analysis.
