MECHANISM: This paper develops a method to combine transition interface sampling simulations using different collective variables by applying multistate Bennett acceptance ratio (MBAR) reweighting to entire trajectories. The mechanism involves computing path weights that account for selection bias in interface ensembles, using likelihood maximization over trajectory space. Trajectories are weighted based on the maximum interface they cross, and partition sums are computed iteratively from the MBAR fixed-point equations. The approach extends to multiple collective variables by constructing a joint likelihood over all biased ensembles, enabling backward reweighting to recover unbiased path ensembles and observables. Validation on potential models and host-guest systems demonstrates improved statistics compared to naive ensemble combination.
