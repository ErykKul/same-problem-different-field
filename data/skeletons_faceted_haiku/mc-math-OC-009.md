MECHANISM: The paper develops variational inference using Gaussian interacting particles evolving in the Bures-Wasserstein geometry. Particles represent approximations to the posterior distribution, with dynamics driven by a gradient descent step that minimizes the Wasserstein distance between the particle ensemble and the target. The algorithm uses interactions among particles to refine the variational approximation through successive projections onto the Gaussian manifold.
