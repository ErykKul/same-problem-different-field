MECHANISM: Implements web-based visualization platforms for light curves using Plotly (frontend), FastAPI (Python backend), and MongoDB (JSON storage, Docker-containerized). Fulu applies neural networks and Gaussian processes to approximate multiband light curves with uneven sampling; VALC applies post-processing algorithms (zero-point/color correction, filtering, median reference subtraction) to forced photometry. Both platforms provide search, filtering, and interactive time-series visualization tools without real-time computational backend processing.
