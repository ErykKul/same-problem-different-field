MECHANISM: Multi-dimensional échellogram features (line centers in 2D space) from laser frequency comb calibration data are tracked over time by fitting shifts, scalings, and rotation parameters. Line bisector span is computed from LFC modes to measure instrumental profile asymmetry. Solar radial velocity observations from two independent instruments are differenced to isolate instrumental systematics. RV measurements from multiple chromospherically quiet stellar targets are binned to reveal global instrumental trends. A multi-dimensional regression model combines these empirical tracers with instrument telemetry to calibrate out coherent instrument drifts.
