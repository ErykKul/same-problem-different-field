MECHANISM: A heterogeneous Monod-Wyman-Changeux (MWC) model of binary receptor cluster state (active/inactive) parameterized by ligand concentration. Three performance metrics are computed: channel capacity via Blahut-Arimoto algorithm (iterative reweighting of input distribution to maximize mutual information), dynamic range (difference between saturated and basal activity states), and effective Hill coefficient (steepness of response at transition midpoint). A seven-dimensional parameter space is swept (allosteric constant, four state-dependent dissociation constants, receptor copy numbers) and results visualized as heatmaps with gradient norms calculated at strain operating points.
