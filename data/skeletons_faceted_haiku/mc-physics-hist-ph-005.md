MECHANISM: A dialogue explores the mathematical foundations of quantum mechanics without reference to physics, deriving the formalism from measurement context and observability constraints. Starting from classical probability on isolated systems (where unobservable states exist), the dialogue constructs a Hilbert space representation using truth-value combinations as basis vectors. Complex numbers are introduced to restore linearity after constraining to observable outcomes. The resulting algebra of projectors on complex spaces is shown to be the necessary mathematical structure for probabilistic frequencies in contexts with unobservability, not a consequence of atomic phenomena. Bell's inequalities are reinterpreted as violations of classical logic when variables lack truth values in certain measurement contexts.
