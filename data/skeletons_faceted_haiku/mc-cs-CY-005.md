MECHANISM: The paper trains ensemble classifiers (Random Forest, Extra Trees) on administrative case records from a prosecutor's office to predict which open cases are likely to be finalized within six months. The system extracts temporal event features, case characteristics, and lawyer activity metrics; classifies each case as likely or unlikely to close; and ranks cases by predicted closure probability to support triage decisions and identify long-inactive cases.
