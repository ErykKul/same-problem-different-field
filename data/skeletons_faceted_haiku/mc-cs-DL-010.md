MECHANISM: This paper presents a large-scale audit of author commitments in peer review by comparing peer review rebuttals against camera-ready paper versions. Large language models are used to extract and match promised changes across two text documents. The system detects unfulfilled commitments by comparing rebuttal text with final paper content. An Author Commitment Checklist is proposed to alert authors and organizers to unaddressed promises. This is primarily a position paper using LLMs as classification tools, not a novel computational method.
