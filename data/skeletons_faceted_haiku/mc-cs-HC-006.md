MECHANISM: A large-scale observational study analyzing 1,484,633 mobile app reviews across 422 applications (200 with AI features, 222 controls) from iOS and Google Play stores. Sentiment classification, topic modeling, and concern-benefit categorization are applied to identify the frequency of AI mentions and their relationship to user ratings and concerns. Hierarchical regression quantifies how controlling for AI recognition reverses the apparent relationship between AI presence and user satisfaction, with differential effects analyzed by application category.
