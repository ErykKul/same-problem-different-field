MECHANISM: A phenomenological critique of wave function realism (WFR), examining whether the wave function is physically real or merely a mathematical representation. The paper surveys the measurement problem in quantum mechanics and various interpretations (Copenhagen, many-worlds, Bohmian mechanics, GRW), analyzing the temptation to "read off" ontology from formalism. It reconsiders the London-Bauer interpretation through phenomenological frameworks and proposes a correlational rather than objectivist notion of realism.
