MECHANISM: A mathematical model describes the evolution of multi-dimensional opinions via binary interactions among agents. Each agent has a d-dimensional opinion vector and importance weights across topics. Interaction is governed by a distance function combining weighted similarity across all topics (with parameter β controlling current vs. other topics). Microscopic interaction rules are formulated as a kinetic equation, whose mean-field limit yields a Vlasov-type PDE. The PDE describes how agent opinion distributions change over time through integrodifferential conservation laws. Analysis establishes existence and non-negativity of solutions via Picard-Lindelöf theorem, and studies stationary states and convergence properties.
