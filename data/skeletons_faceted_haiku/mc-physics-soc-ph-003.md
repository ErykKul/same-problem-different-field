MECHANISM: A tutorial on formal mathematical modeling for understanding cultural evolution. Surveys four families of models: optimization-based reasoning (Bayesian inference, game theory), adaptive updating (reinforcement learning, evolutionary games), mean-field approaches (compartmental, population dynamics), and complex systems (agent-based, social networks). Develops a general template connecting system states, cognitive processes, behavior, and macro outcomes through dynamic loops. Discusses the spectrum from analytical to computational models and the tradeoff between precision and tractability.
