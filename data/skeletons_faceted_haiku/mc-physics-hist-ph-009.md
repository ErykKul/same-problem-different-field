MECHANISM: A philosophical analysis of the failure to generalize the classical work concept to quantum thermodynamics. The paper diagnoses a no-go theorem showing that two intuitive classical features of work (energy-difference equivalence and operationality via two-point measurements) cannot jointly hold in quantum regimes. The incompatibility is attributed to the measurement problem and decoherence. An alternative trajectory-based definition using the quantum potential is proposed and examined for whether it preserves classical consilience.
