MECHANISM: The paper develops scalable algorithms for mean field control problems with kernel-based interactions using random Fourier features. The mean field limit approximates a large population of interacting agents. Random Fourier features reduce dimensionality by approximating kernel functions via low-rank random expansions, enabling efficient computation of the mean field coupling. Control optimization uses value iteration or policy gradient on the reduced-dimensional problem.
