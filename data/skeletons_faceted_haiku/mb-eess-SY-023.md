MECHANISM: A bi-level game-theoretic model is formulated in which a regulator acts as the leader, setting price caps and feed-in tariffs to maximize household economic surplus subject to maintaining a monopoly operator's current profitability. The operator (follower) responds by choosing generation, access, and purchasing decisions to maximize its profit. The regulator's payoff aggregates consumer demand satisfaction and renewable energy feed-in value; the operator's payoff is the discounted net present value of revenues minus capital, fixed, and variable costs. The bi-level problem is solved to find the regulator's policy that induces an operator response balancing renewable integration with profit incentives.
