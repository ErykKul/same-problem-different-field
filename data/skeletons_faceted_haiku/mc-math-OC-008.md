MECHANISM: The paper develops algorithms for maximizing weakly diminishing-returns submodular functions over down-closed convex bodies. Two algorithms are combined via a convex mixture: a gamma-aware Frank-Wolfe-guided measured continuous greedy algorithm that balances ascent along greedy directions with measured updates, and a gamma-aware double-greedy algorithm that adjusts upper and lower bound solutions. The final guarantee is optimized by tuning parameters to maximize an objective-dependent performance curve. Analyses use case-based progress tracking for asymmetric marginal decay properties.
