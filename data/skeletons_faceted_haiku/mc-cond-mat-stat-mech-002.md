MECHANISM: This paper studies Anderson localization on random graphs embedded with spatial structure through exponentially-decaying hopping amplitudes. The mechanism involves constructing a random regular graph (RRG) with all-to-all hopping that decays exponentially with graph distance, balancing exponential growth of neighbors at distant sites against exponential decay of hopping amplitude. The analysis combines exact diagonalization computing level spacing ratios and inverse participation ratios, with analytical self-consistent renormalized perturbation theory to determine local self-energies. The method maps disorder-driven transitions between ergodic and localized phases, revealing competing mechanisms controlled by two parameters: hopping decay length-scale and disorder strength. A scaling analysis based on generalized inverse participation ratios probes multifractal structure versus direct phase transitions.
