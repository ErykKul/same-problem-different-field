MECHANISM: A nonlinear deterministic observer estimates orientation on the Lie group SO(3) using scalar measurements (e.g., individual components of vector observations or scalar constraints from alternative modalities) along with gyroscope signals. The observer incorporates gyroscope bias compensation and is grounded in a Riccati-based framework that governs both dynamics and Lyapunov-based stability. The approach avoids embedding SO(3) into higher-dimensional Euclidean space, instead working directly on the manifold. Observability conditions and persistent excitation requirements are derived to guarantee local exponential stability under partial measurements.
DOMAIN: Attitude estimation and inertial navigation
