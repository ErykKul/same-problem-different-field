MECHANISM: This paper reviews theoretical frameworks for understanding topological properties in gapless quantum critical systems. The approach generalizes topology beyond gapped phases by analyzing edge states in systems where the bulk energy gap closes. The mechanism involves mapping quantum critical points to classical systems using quantum-classical correspondence, applying conformal field theory (CFT) to extract critical exponents and universality classes, and using renormalization group techniques to compute scaling behavior. The paper discusses symmetry-breaking and symmetry-protected topological phases, both noninteracting and interacting regimes, and extends these frameworks to unconventional quantum critical points that violate traditional mappings.
