MECHANISM: An evolutionary game model simulates population dynamics using the replicator equation. Agents adopt one of three pure strategies: unconditional cooperation, unconditional defection, or a conditional strategy combining three rules (reward after receiving help, reward selective recipients with good reputation, punish bad-reputation recipients). In each round, pairs interact in a one-shot helping game where donors choose to help, incurring cost c while providing benefit b. Reputations (Good or Bad) update according to strict assessment rules. The system evolves via replicator dynamics where strategies with above-average payoffs increase in frequency. Equilibrium analysis determines stable mixtures and welfare outcomes by comparing population-average payoffs with and without costly punishment.
