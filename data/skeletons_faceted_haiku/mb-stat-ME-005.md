MECHANISM: A hierarchical model forecasts cause-specific mortality counts while respecting the compositional constraint that causes sum to total deaths. Age-specific and cause-specific mortality is modeled using a joint Dirichlet-Multinomial-Poisson framework. The Dirichlet-Multinomial captures the composition of deaths across causes (proportions), while the Poisson models total deaths by age. Model parameters are estimated from historical mortality data. Time trends in cause-specific proportions are captured via time-varying Dirichlet parameters. Forecasts are generated by projecting trend components and sampling from the posterior predictive distribution. The framework ensures that forecasts are internally coherent: cause-specific counts automatically sum to total mortality.
