MECHANISM: An agent-based geospatial simulation framework models daily human mobility, social contacts, and toilet usage within urban environments. Each simulated entity follows a physiologically motivated defecation cycle and frequents specific locations. When infection occurs, a pathogen shedding model determines fecal load. The epidemic model tracks disease transmission through spatial and social interactions. The framework couples behavioral, transmission, and pathogen-shedding sub-models to generate spatial-temporal wastewater contamination signals that are compared to residential population assumptions.
