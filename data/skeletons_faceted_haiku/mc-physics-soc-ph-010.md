MECHANISM: A flow-network cascade model tracks how individual entity overloads propagate through interconnected systems via redirection of flow to neighbors. Initial external shocks initiate cascades; accumulated load on entities determines failure thresholds. The model derives a transformation law connecting input tail exponents (Pareto distributed external shocks) to output tail exponents (cascade sizes). Analysis identifies conditions under which heavy-tailed failure distributions emerge and establishes a unified framework applicable across power grids, traffic networks, and processing systems.
