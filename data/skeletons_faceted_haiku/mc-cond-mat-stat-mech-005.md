MECHANISM: This paper derives the Parisi free energy formula for the Sherrington-Kirkpatrick spin glass model via cavity method by assuming the equilibrium state is a product of independent Random Energy Models (REMs). The mechanism decomposes the full spin system into exponentially many independent subsystems, representing the measure as a factored product over subsystem Gibbs measures. Cavity field and correction variables are decomposed across subsystems using Gaussian random matrix analysis. For each subsystem modeled as a REM with Poisson point process structure, the special averaging properties of PPP (Little's theorem) enable recursive computation of contributions. The final functional emerges from cancellation of REM normalization factors, recovering exact Parisi formula.
