MECHANISM: Theoretical analysis of culture as an interdependent system where traits (beliefs, practices, artifacts) are embedded in cognitive, social, and material structures. Traits interact through functional compatibility, symbolic association, and prerequisite relations, generating path dependence and tension. Cultural learning is characterized as coherence-seeking information processing where dissonance reduction drives trait combinations. Higher-order emergent filters (goals, norms, skills) regulate subsequent evolution. Systemic tensions accumulate until paradigm-like shifts reconfigure the entire trait network.
