MECHANISM: Analyze recurrent events in the presence of terminal events (death) that censor future recurrences. Decompose treatment effects into two separable components: direct effect on recurrent events and indirect effect mediated through survival. Estimate counterfactual mean recurrent event functions using G-formula with weights that adjust the risk set to eliminate survival-related bias. Formulate a marginal structural model where the counterfactual mean function follows a proportional rate form: multiplicative treatment effect constant over time. Conduct a score-based hypothesis test comparing observed and counterfactual recurrent event increments across time intervals.
