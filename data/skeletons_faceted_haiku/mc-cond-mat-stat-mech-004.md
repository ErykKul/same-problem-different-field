MECHANISM: This paper establishes a mathematical connection between two distinct methods for identifying Hilbert space fragmentation (HSF) in quantum systems. The mechanism involves computing characteristic polynomial factorization of Hamiltonian matrices over rational numbers (ICPF method) and comparing this to decomposition via commutant algebra irreducible representations (CA method). The central theorem proves: if all rational operators in the center of the commutant algebra have rational eigenvalues, then ICPF yields an equal or finer fragmentation than CA. The approach uses spectral analysis of projection operators, polynomial uniqueness arguments, and Galois theory to establish relationships between polynomial factors and Krylov subspace dimensions. Numerical verification on models including pair-flip, Temperley-Lieb, and quantum breakdown demonstrates the theorem.
