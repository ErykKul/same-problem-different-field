MECHANISM: A randomized controlled field experiment assigns participants to three treatment groups and a control group. Each treatment group receives a distinct informational intervention: cost-based (financial incentives, fuel savings), range-based (vehicle range, charging infrastructure), or norm-based (social proof via early adopter story). The outcome is an electric vehicle perception score where respondents allocate 100 points between conventional and electric vehicles pre- and post-intervention. Treatment effects are estimated via ordinary least squares regression with demographic controls. Binary logistic regression tests whether changes in perception scores correspond to shifts in vehicle preference.
