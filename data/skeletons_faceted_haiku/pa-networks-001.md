MECHANISM: An analytic model computes the degree distribution of a network that grows by adding edges only (vertices fixed) under preferential attachment rules: at each step, a new edge is added between two entities with probability proportional to a power of their degree. The analysis uses generating functions and recurrence relations to derive closed-form expressions for the distribution of entity degrees. Linear and non-linear attachment regimes are treated separately. The key step is taking large-time and large-degree limits to reveal the asymptotic functional form of the distribution.
