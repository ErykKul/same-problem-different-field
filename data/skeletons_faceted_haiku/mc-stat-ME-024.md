MECHANISM: A multivariate autoregressive model on a known network embeds nodes in a low-rank variable subspace. The transition matrix has a separable bilinear structure: fixed network topology coupled with learnable low-rank factors. Scaled gradient descent optimization updates the low-rank parameters. Non-asymptotic error bounds are derived under a network-specific distance metric.
DOMAIN: Network analysis, multivariate time series, high-dimensional statistics
STRUCTURE: sparse linear algebra
DATA_OBJECT: graph or network
INFERENCE: frequentist point estimate
PROBLEM_FORM: estimation
DISTRIBUTION: none
COMPLEXITY: finite-sample bound
