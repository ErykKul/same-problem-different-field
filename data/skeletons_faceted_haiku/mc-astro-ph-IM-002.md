MECHANISM: Characterizes spectral performance of plastic scintillator bars (coupled to multi-anode PMTs via MAROC-3A ASIC) and GAGG scintillator bars (coupled to avalanche photodiodes via SKIROC-2A ASIC) for a Compton polarimeter. For each detector type, measures response curves using radioactive sources (Fe-55, Am-241, Cd-109, Co-57, Eu-155, I-129) across energy ranges 1–100 keV. Energy linearity, resolution, dynamic range, optical crosstalk, and pile-up effects are extracted from spectral fits and noise-rate measurements. Coincidence logic verified between scatterer and absorber chains.
