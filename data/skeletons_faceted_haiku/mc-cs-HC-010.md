MECHANISM: An empirical user study with 59 children (ages 8-10) examining behavioral responses to successive robot communication failures. Participants interact with a robot that fails to understand three prompts in succession, with behavioral responses video-recorded and qualitatively analyzed. Reactions are compared to prior adult responses from the same failure paradigm to identify age-related differences in error handling strategies, emotional responses, and engagement levels.
