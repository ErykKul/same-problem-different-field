MECHANISM: A system of coupled ordinary differential equations models the dynamics of concentrations in a biological oscillator network subject to periodic external forcing. Analysis characterizes bifurcations by studying how the amplitude and period of oscillations change with control parameters. Numerical simulation traces trajectories in parameter space and identifies the critical value where a qualitative change in dynamics occurs. Linear analysis of steady states and Hopf bifurcation theory prove the mechanism of the transition.
