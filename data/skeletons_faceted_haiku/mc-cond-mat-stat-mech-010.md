MECHANISM: This paper studies skyrmion phase stability under mechanical strain using a competing-interactions magnetic model. The mechanism involves computing ground state configurations and phase boundaries via minimization of a magnetic Hamiltonian containing exchange, Dzyaloshinskii-Moriya, and anisotropy terms. Strain is incorporated through strain-dependent exchange anisotropy coupling to spatial deformation. Phase transitions between skyrmion-lattice, skyrmion-fluid, and paramagnetic phases are identified via energy gap collapse and order parameter divergence. Monte Carlo simulations with temperature variation map phase diagrams under different strain magnitudes.
