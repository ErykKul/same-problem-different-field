MECHANISM: A sample of 21 water megamaser disk AGN with published supermassive black hole masses and distances is compiled. For each source, the predicted black hole shadow angular size is calculated from mass and distance using a Schwarzschild metric formula. New Submillimeter Array continuum observations at 230 GHz are conducted, supplemented by archival ALMA and VLA data. Continuum flux densities are extracted via Gaussian fitting. Baseline requirements for interferometric resolution of each source's shadow are computed using the diffraction limit. Astrometric precision requirements are evaluated by comparing maser dynamical center uncertainties with BHS centroid precision needs.
