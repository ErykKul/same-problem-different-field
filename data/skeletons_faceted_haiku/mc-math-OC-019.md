MECHANISM: Given an underactuated nonlinear system and a desired equilibrium point, construct an energy function and feedback matrices that satisfy a system of partial differential equations (the matching equation). The method introduces maximum energy shapeability, which ensures the existence of characteristic coordinates. These enable a coordinate transformation that reduces the matching equation to a lower-dimensional PDE in terms of a subset of variables. The reduced PDE can be solved by applying the Mean Value Theorem for vector-valued functions, combined with integrability conditions on the Hessian of the energy function. The control law is derived from the resulting energy function and feedback matrices. Sufficient conditions are provided to ensure maximum energy shapeability holds for certain system structures.
DOMAIN: Control theory, nonlinear systems, passivity-based control
