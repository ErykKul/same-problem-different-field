MECHANISM: A quantum-classical hybrid framework constructs fermionic Hamiltonians from molecular structure data, applies Jordan-Wigner transformation to map to qubit operators, and optimizes ground state energies using variational quantum eigensolver with parameter shift rule gradients and adaptive learning rates. The algorithm exhibits three convergence phases: exponential decay of one-body terms, power-law optimization of two-body Coulomb interactions, and asymptotic fine-tuning of correlation effects. Practical applications include predicting molecular binding energies and metabolism sites by solving small active-space electronic structure problems.
