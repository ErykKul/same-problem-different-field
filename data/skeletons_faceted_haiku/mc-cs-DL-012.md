MECHANISM: This paper introduces a literature-driven framework for dataset discovery that extracts citation contexts from scientific papers, uses large language models with schema-guided recognition to identify datasets mentioned in citations, and performs entity resolution to preserve provenance. The system combines citation-context extraction, LLM-based entity recognition, and matching against existing dataset registries to retrieve datasets based on actual research use rather than metadata. Large-scale citation context mining enables dataset discovery but primarily applies existing techniques rather than introducing novel computational methods.
