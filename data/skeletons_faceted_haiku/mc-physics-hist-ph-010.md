MECHANISM: A philosophical critique arguing that causality is not a fundamental principle of nature but a practical shorthand emergent at macroscopic scales. The paper analyzes how strict microcausality is not essential for quantum field theory's success and discusses various approaches to quantum gravity that violate causality (fakeons, nonlocal theories) while maintaining unitarity and renormalizability. It concludes that true causality and strict predictivity are impossible in principle, replaced by retrospective "prepostdictions" constrained by statistics.
