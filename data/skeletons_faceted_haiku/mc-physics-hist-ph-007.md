MECHANISM: Pilot-wave theory is extended with non-Hermitian Hamiltonians by complexifying the electromagnetic coupling parameter to enforce local scale invariance alongside local phase invariance. The scale factor is parallel-transported as a path-dependent line integral along classical trajectories in configuration space. The conserved probability density becomes a trajectory-dependent ratio of wave function amplitude squared to the transported scale. Weyl covariant derivatives act on both the wave function amplitude and the scale factor. The theory is generalized from single particles to multiple particles, spin-1/2 systems, Dirac particles, and quantum fields, preserving gauge invariance and yielding unique, trajectory-dependent probability predictions.
