MECHANISM: An extended compartmental disease model distinguishes original and mutant strains with vaccination compartments. Vaccination decisions follow evolutionary game theory where entities choose strategies based on neighbor choices and epidemiological state, parameterized by vaccine cost and efficacy. The epidemic and behavioral processes couple via microscopic Markov chain approximation, yielding time-varying vaccination rates. Numerical simulations integrate the coupled system to analyze outbreak trajectories and sensitivity of vaccination adoption to parameter changes.
