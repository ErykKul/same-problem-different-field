MECHANISM: An information-theoretic framework quantifies structural redundancy in hypergraph representations of networked systems. The method identifies critical higher-order interactions (hyperedges) whose removal minimally degrades essential structural information. Information content is measured via entropy or divergence metrics on state distributions induced by hypergraph topology. The algorithm selects hyperedges iteratively, removing those with minimal information loss until achieving a parsimonious reduced representation capturing the system's essential multi-scale structure.
