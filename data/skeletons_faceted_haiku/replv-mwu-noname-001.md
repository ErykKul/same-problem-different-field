MECHANISM: In the experts' problem, a player repeatedly chooses which expert to follow, seeing costs ex post. Compute optimal play strategies by tracking the cumulative-loss gap between experts. For two experts, use dynamic programming to find the optimal probability distribution over experts at each round given the current gap. Equivalently, solve a continuous-time analog via Brownian motion and stochastic calculus, yielding a closed-form solution.
DOMAIN: Online learning; prediction with expert advice; regret bounds
STRUCTURE: Dynamic programming or other: Brownian motion/stochastic calculus
