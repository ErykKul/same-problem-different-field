MECHANISM: The generalized Hurst exponent (GHE) framework measures how q-th order moments of absolute returns scale with time aggregation τ. The method computes E[|r_τ(t)|^q] across multiple time horizons and fits power-law scaling exponents H(q) for each moment order. Uniscaling behavior (H(q) constant) is distinguished from multiscaling (H(q) varying with q). The approach applies this analysis to rough Bergomi log-price processes and empirical financial time series to characterize whether observed return scaling arises from temporal dependencies or distributional heavy-tailedness. Comparison with synthetic processes generated under different assumptions helps isolate sources of multiscaling.
