MECHANISM: The model couples two interconnected networks where entities in one layer engage in pairwise cost-benefit interactions with two states (cooperate or defect), while in the second layer entities compete between two types under constant fitness differences. Payoffs aggregate across layers to guide state transitions via death-birth updating rules. The analysis derives fixation probabilities under weak selection to characterize when cooperators vs mutants are favored, examines random-walk-based network metrics that depend on topology, and exhaustively classifies networks with six entities to determine prevalence of cooperation outcomes.
DOMAIN: Evolutionary game theory, social networks, cooperation dynamics
STRUCTURE: Graph traversal
DATA_OBJECT: Graph or network
INFERENCE: Optimization only

DISTRIBUTION: none
