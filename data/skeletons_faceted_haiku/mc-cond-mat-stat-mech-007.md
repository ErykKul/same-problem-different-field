MECHANISM: This paper studies ergodic and chaotic properties of Clifford quantum cellular automata (QCAs) by establishing a correspondence with classical linear cellular automata over finite fields. The mechanism maps translationally-invariant Clifford QCAs to pseudo-unitary matrix actions on Laurent polynomial spaces, reducing quantum problems to classical ones. Thermalization is analyzed via diffusivity: strongly diffusive QCAs exhibit unbounded growth of operator support, guaranteeing thermalization; weakly diffusive QCAs thermalize states for almost all times (density-1 subsets of time indices). The analysis distinguishes between weak and strong thermalization, applies r-mixing theory, and proves that quantum and classical ergodic hierarchies collapse for Clifford systems.
