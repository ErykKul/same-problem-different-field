MECHANISM: A nonlinear system of five coupled differential equations models unemployment dynamics with five state variables: unskilled unemployed, skilled unemployed, employed, discouraged workers, and job vacancies. Each compartment has rates of transition determined by bilinear interaction terms between populations and parameters capturing skill-development, discouragement, and job matching. Equilibrium points are derived by solving a polynomial system (an 8th-degree polynomial in skilled unemployment). Stability is analyzed via eigenvalue computation and Routh-Hurwitz criterion. Numerical solutions are obtained using fourth-order Runge-Kutta method. The region of attraction (bounded invariant set) is proven analytically.
DOMAIN: Labor economics, unemployment dynamics, epidemiological modeling
STRUCTURE: dynamic programming

INFERENCE: optimization only
PROBLEM_FORM: characterization or proof
