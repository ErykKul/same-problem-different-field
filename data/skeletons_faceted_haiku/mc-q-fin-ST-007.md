MECHANISM: The paper applies Tikhonov-regularized deconvolution to extract impulse response functions showing how order flow from different investor classes affects asset prices over time. For each investor type, the deconvolution recovers a kernel of coefficients describing price impact at lags 0-60 days from a linear convolution model. Hawkes process analysis characterizes trading surges as self-exciting point events, with branching ratio measuring criticality. The system partitions the data into high-herding and normal regimes using Hawkes intensity thresholds. Regime-conditional deconvolution estimates separate impulse responses for each regime. Entropy production rates quantify information-theoretic irreversibility in flow-return dynamics. Hawkes memory clustering analyzes inter-event temporal structure. The methodology tests cross-sectional patterns of institutional price impact by firm size.
