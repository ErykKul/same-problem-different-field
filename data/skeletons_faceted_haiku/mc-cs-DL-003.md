MECHANISM: This is an empirical study that analyzes the distribution of scholarly and policy references within 17.5 million indexed policy documents from the Overton database. The study retrieves bibliographic metadata (publication year, source, country, language, subject area, topic) and reference data, then computes three descriptive metrics (total, coverage, geometric mean) for both scholarly and policy references. These metrics are aggregated and compared across multiple dimensions. The paper uses statistical description and distributional analysis but introduces no novel computational method.
