MECHANISM: Model a low-voltage distribution network with distributed photovoltaic (PV) systems managed by smart inverters (SIs) under heterogeneous control modes. Define SI control curves (piecewise linear droop characteristics) for Volt-VAR and Volt-Watt modes that adjust reactive and active power as functions of measured local voltage. Simulate the coupled network dynamics using ordinary differential equations representing power flows, voltage relationships, and control feedback loops. Execute real-time simulations on Opal-RT hardware-in-the-loop platform to evaluate how control mode changes in one SI group affect the dynamic power and voltage response of other groups.
