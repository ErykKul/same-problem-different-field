MECHANISM: A latent-state POMDP is formulated where a robot infers and shapes a human's unobserved prosocial state through repeated interactions. The robot learns transition and observation models via expectation-maximization from user study data. The robot computes a belief-based policy using dynamic programming that selects actions (helping or signaling) to maximize long-term cooperation by balancing immediate task outcomes against the expected value of future interactions. The policy learns the causal relationship between its actions and the evolution of the human's prosocial inclination.
DOMAIN: Human-robot interaction and social learning
STRUCTURE: Dynamic programming
DATA_OBJECT: sequence or time-series
INFERENCE: Bayesian posterior
