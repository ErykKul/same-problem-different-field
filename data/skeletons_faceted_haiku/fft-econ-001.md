MECHANISM: Computes the spectral decomposition of a univariate time series to classify its frequency-domain properties. Estimates the spectral distribution function for a stationary time series and develops statistical propositions for patterns of deviation from white noise. Classifies time series into three categories based on which frequency bands stochastically dominate others. For each class, applies statistical tests with confidence intervals to identify whether low-frequency components (compounding effects), high-frequency components (mean-reversion), or mixed-frequency components (mixed complexity) drive temporal dynamics. Empirically applies this framework to multiple observables across sectors.
DOMAIN: Time series analysis, frequency domain econometrics, business cycles
STRUCTURE: spectral or transform
DATA_OBJECT: sequence or time-series
INFERENCE: frequentist point estimate
