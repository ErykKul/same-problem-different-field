MECHANISM: Design and compare three exact mathematical formulations for routing electric vehicles with time windows and battery constraints. First formulation uses event-based networks where nodes represent (location, onboard-customer-set) tuples and arcs represent feasible vehicle state transitions. Second formulation adds discrete time indices to the network while maintaining continuous battery variables, enabling fragment-based decomposition. Third formulation discretizes both time and battery dimensions into finite index sets. The approach systematically isolates the computational effects of discretization by controlled comparison between formulations. Integer linear programming with branch-and-cut and branch-and-price solution methods.
