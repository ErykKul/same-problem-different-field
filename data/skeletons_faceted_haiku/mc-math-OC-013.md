MECHANISM: The paper develops gradient-free (zeroth-order) optimization methods for problems with Markovian noise, where the source of randomness has temporal correlation rather than being independent. Methods use finite-difference estimates or other black-box gradient surrogates and analyze convergence under Markov dependence in the noise process. Analysis tracks how Markovian memory affects convergence rates compared to i.i.d. noise.
