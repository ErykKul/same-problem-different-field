MECHANISM: Origin-destination demand and mode-choice parameters are jointly estimated from multi-source system-level data (traffic counts, speeds, transit ridership, trajectories) using a computational graph framework. The model is solved via maximum likelihood or gradient-based optimization on the computation graph, with hypothesis tests derived to assess parameter significance and model fit.
