MECHANISM: Systematic parametric experimentation with laser-matter interaction under controlled atmospheric conditions. Single-track and two-dimensional samples are fabricated by sequentially melting metal powder layers with a focused laser beam while varying laser power, scanning speed, and hatch spacing. Surface morphology, structural cohesion, and oxidation are evaluated across three atmospheric environments using optical microscopy, scanning electron microscopy, and energy-dispersive X-ray spectroscopy to characterize compositional differences.
