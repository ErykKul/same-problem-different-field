MECHANISM: Acquires resting-state EEG signals and decomposes them into frequency bands via Fourier transform. Computes multiple spectral features for each band: relative power by normalization, spectral entropy from normalized power distribution, and phase synchronization indices via Hilbert transform. Calculates the instantaneous phase of filtered signals, then quantifies phase coherence between electrode pairs. Compares these three feature types between an Alzheimer disease group and normal controls via statistical tests, and correlates features with cognitive test scores.
