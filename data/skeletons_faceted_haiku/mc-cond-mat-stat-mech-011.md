MECHANISM: This paper studies phase transitions and critical behavior in a non-Hermitian Ising-Gamma model with off-diagonal exchange interactions and dissipative loss/gain terms. The mechanism involves exact diagonalization of the non-Hermitian Hamiltonian using Jordan block decomposition or similarity transformations to find eigenvalues and eigenstates in presence of complex energy shift from dissipation. Phase boundaries are identified via energy gap closure and order parameters measured from expectation values. Critical exponents are extracted from finite-size scaling of entanglement entropy and order parameters near phase transitions.
