MECHANISM: The paper applies correlation integral analysis to characterize the dimensionality and complexity of time series generated by stochastic processes. For a sequence of observations embedded in m-dimensional space, the correlation integral computes the fraction of point pairs whose sup-norm separation is at most r. The approach tests whether observed data follow strictly stationary, absolutely regular processes. Correlation integrals are estimated empirically and compared against theoretical properties under independence and varying time-delay embedding dimensions. The methodology determines whether apparent complexity arises from genuine temporal structure or from distributional properties, using asymptotic normality and permutation testing for statistical inference.
