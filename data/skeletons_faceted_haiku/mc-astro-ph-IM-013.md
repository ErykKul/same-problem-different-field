MECHANISM: Nanophase and bulk enstatite are synthesized via sol-gel methods with controlled annealing temperatures. Particle morphology is characterized using electron microscopy, X-ray diffraction, and surface area analysis to determine crystallite sizes and surface areas at multiple length scales. High-temperature oxide melt solution calorimetry measures enthalpies of the nanoparticles and bulk phases. Linear regression on the measured excess enthalpies against calculated surface and interface areas determines surface energy and interfacial energy per unit area.
DOMAIN: Materials thermodynamics; mineral surface chemistry; oxide calorimetry
STRUCTURE: sparse linear algebra
DATA_OBJECT: dense matrix or tensor
INFERENCE: frequentist point estimate
PROBLEM_FORM: estimation
DISTRIBUTION: none
