MECHANISM: A methodological framework is proposed for studying binary star evolution using volume-complete samples from Gaia parallax data. Space densities and occurrence rates of progenitor systems are compared with post-interaction systems to constrain binary evolution parameters. Selection criteria are outlined for different binary populations. A scheduler algorithm concept is described for optimizing observation timing of radial velocity variations across multiple binary populations in next-generation spectroscopic surveys.
