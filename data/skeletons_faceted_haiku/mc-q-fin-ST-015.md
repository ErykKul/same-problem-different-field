MECHANISM: The methodology defines variance timescales as weight vectors that minimize the drift of variance in a multivariate process subject to unit variance constraints. This leads to a generalized eigenvalue problem where instantaneous drift is minimized while preserving variance. The solution yields ordered principal directions ranked by their variance stabilization properties. The approach is applied to identify time-scale hierarchies in multivariate price or return processes, determining which combinations of asset movements exhibit stable vs. drifting variance profiles over different observation periods. The framework uses spectral analysis (eigenvector decomposition) to characterize the temporal organization of volatility across the cross-section of assets.
