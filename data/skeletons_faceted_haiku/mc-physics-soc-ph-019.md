MECHANISM: An iterative algorithm computes self-consistent node measures on bipartite networks, where measures in one set depend on measures in the other set and vice versa. Each iteration updates node values by aggregating weighted contributions from adjacent nodes in the opposite set, followed by normalization. The paper derives a convergence criterion based on network structure: the system converges when the product of multiplicities along the Z-shaped diagonal exceeds the product along the unconnected diagonal. For non-convergent networks, three remedies are provided: removing low-degree nodes, merging problematic nodes, or adding a regularization constant to iteration equations.
