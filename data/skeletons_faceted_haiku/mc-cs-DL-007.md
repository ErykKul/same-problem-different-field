MECHANISM: This paper is a bibliometric analysis of research on Fibrodysplasia Ossificans Progressiva covering 1989-2023. It retrieves publication metadata from academic databases, extracts bibliometric indicators (publication counts, citation counts, h-index, collaboration patterns), and computes summary statistics and trend analyses across publication year, author affiliation, and country. The analysis employs standard descriptive statistics and visualization but introduces no novel computational method.
