MECHANISM: This paper establishes rigorous theoretical results for the emergence of Scrooge ensembles (maximal entropy distributions of pure states under physical constraints) in quantum many-body systems. The mechanism involves analyzing temporal and projected ensembles through the framework of Scrooge k-designs, which approximate Scrooge ensembles by matching their first k statistical moments. The approach includes: proving that chaotic unitary dynamics alone generate approximate global Scrooge designs without measurement, showing that measuring a complementary subsystem of a scrambled state induces local Scrooge designs, and demonstrating that scrambled measurement bases can generate Scrooge behavior from arbitrary entangled states. Numerical simulations identify coherence, entanglement, magic (non-stabilizerness), and information scrambling as essential resources for emergence.
