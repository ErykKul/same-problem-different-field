MECHANISM: A bi-level metamodel framework couples a response function mapping intervention parameters to health outcomes with Gaussian process regression learning spatial and socio-economic structures of heterogeneous treatment effects. Initial sampling of counties and intervention conditions is random; sequential design uses spatial correlations and posterior uncertainty to adaptively select the most informative configurations for simulation. The metamodel is fit to outputs from a calibrated agent-based disease simulator, achieving efficient surrogate prediction with one-tenth the simulation evaluations of exhaustive enumeration.
