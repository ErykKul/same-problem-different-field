MECHANISM: This is an experimental fabrication study with no computational method of its own. Physical specimens are produced under varied process settings and surrounding-gas conditions, and the resulting samples are inspected and compared. The only quantitative operation is a ratio: a measured filled area is divided by a target area to give a retention percentage, and a derived energy-density quantity is computed as a power divided by a speed. Outcomes are assessed by imaging and elemental inspection and summarized qualitatively across conditions. No model, estimator, simulation, or proof is involved.
