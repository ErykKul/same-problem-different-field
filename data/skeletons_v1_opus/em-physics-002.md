MECHANISM: One observes a large collection of geometric primitives, each a line in space characterized by a base point and a direction, and posits that each primitive was generated by one of several latent sources plus a uniform background component for spurious primitives. A generative model assigns to each source a center and an isotropic scale, and the probability of a primitive given a source decays exponentially in the squared point-to-line distance, yielding a finite mixture density over the space of primitives. The joint log-likelihood over all primitives is the sum of logs of weighted mixture sums and cannot be maximized directly because of high dimensionality and many local optima. An auxiliary latent assignment variable is introduced for each primitive indicating its generating component, and a soft responsibility is computed as the normalized contribution of each component to that primitive. Iterating, one alternates an expectation step that recomputes responsibilities from the current parameters with a maximization step that updates each component center as a responsibility-weighted projection-matrix-corrected centroid, updates each scale as a responsibility-weighted mean squared distance, and updates each mixing weight as the average responsibility. The number of components is set larger than the expected number of sources, and components whose scale exceeds a threshold are treated as absorbing spurious primitives. To recover time-varying centers, primitives are batched by timestamp and each center is replaced by a low-order polynomial in time whose coefficients are inferred, with the weighted-centroid update generalized to a small linear system per component. Sequential processing across batches uses the previous solution as the initialization for the next, accelerating convergence and naturally maintaining component identity across batches. The output is a set of estimated source centers, scales, and relative weights, optionally with velocity and acceleration coefficients, assembled into trajectories.
