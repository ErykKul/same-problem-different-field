MECHANISM: The work describes the construction and in-flight behavior of a measuring apparatus that disperses an incoming signal into many spatially resolved channels and records the result on a large array of detector elements. No new computational procedure is derived; the quantitative content is limited to back-of-envelope arithmetic relating apparatus geometry to achievable resolution and channel count. Given an aperture size, a focal ratio, and a per-element resolution, the expected resolving power and field coverage are obtained by simple ratios and products. Counts of background and contaminating signal are estimated by multiplying an assumed incident rate by an effective collecting area and a solid angle. Performance before and after hardware modifications is reported as measured scalar quantities rather than computed from a model. The bulk of the document is a descriptive engineering and operations account with design trade-offs stated qualitatively.
