MECHANISM: This is a survey that reviews and qualitatively compares several existing recognition pipelines that map an acoustic signal to discrete symbolic units, contrasting reported accuracy of a layered nonlinear feature-mapping front end, a hand-built feature-table representation, and a feature-enhancement step, each feeding a sequential probabilistic decoder; it introduces no new method of its own and only tabulates previously published performance numbers.
