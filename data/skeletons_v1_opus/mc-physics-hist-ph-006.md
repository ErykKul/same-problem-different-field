MECHANISM: The work establishes a closed-form correspondence between the discrete basis of an operator's eigenstates on an infinite-dimensional function space and a family of nested invariant submanifolds of a two-dimensional continuous state space. A single continuous state is encoded as one complex coordinate, in which the conserved generator becomes a bilinear quantity and the flow is a uniform rotation given by exponentiating a first-order vector field. A cyclic finite group acting by rotations through a rational fraction of a full turn is imposed, and one seeks trajectories invariant under it; the quotient by this group is realized concretely by the power map raising the coordinate to the integer order, which sends the base space onto a cone whose opening angle is the reciprocal of that order. Restricting the flat metric to this quotient and rewriting it in the powered coordinate shows the geometry is conformally flat away from a single fixed point, where the curvature concentrates as a delta contribution proportional to the deficit angle. The same power map carries the rotation generator into a rescaled generator, so each integer power index labels an invariant motion whose frequency and conserved quantity are multiplied by that index. On the operator side, the powered coordinates are exactly the orthogonal eigenfunctions under a Gaussian-weighted inner product, with eigenvalues linear in the index plus a fixed offset from bundle curvature. A general superposition is then reinterpreted as a probability assignment over which invariant submanifold the continuous trajectory occupies. Extending the index to non-integer real values yields multivalued, non-normalizable solutions that remain valid continuous coordinates but leave the function space.
