MECHANISM: A small number of entities are placed under many controlled environmental settings and instrumented to record paired input conditions and physiological output fluxes over time, the data being assembled and released as a reusable reference collection rather than as a new estimator; net exchange is obtained from an inlet-minus-outlet mass balance, a transferred quantity is obtained from the slope of a linear fit to serial weight readings, three-dimensional structure is reconstructed manually from scanned point clouds and validated against direct area measurements, and standard existing response-curve models are fitted to estimate their parameters for downstream calibration.
