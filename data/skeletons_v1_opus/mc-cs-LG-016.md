MECHANISM: Each entity is a location carrying low-dimensional metadata and an associated sequence-valued observation, grouped into several independent replicate collections sharing a common index set. A probabilistic encoder maps each conditioned observation to the parameters of a diagonal latent distribution, and a decoder reconstructs the observation from a latent code conditioned on the metadata; both are trained by maximizing a variational lower bound combining a reconstruction term and a divergence-to-prior penalty. Because joint training across replicates yields misaligned per-replicate latent regions, a separate model is trained per replicate with an added hinge penalty that pulls the latent means at a small set of shared anchor indices toward fixed reference codes, enforcing a common latent geometry. To impute latent codes at unobserved indices in a new replicate, a feature vector is formed by concatenating the latent codes of an index's nearest neighbors, and a multi-output nonparametric regressor with zero-mean priors and independent kernels per output dimension is fit by sparse variational inference to predict latent coordinates from these features. Predicted latent coordinates are then passed through the replicate-specific decoder to generate full reconstructed sequences at all indices. Performance is evaluated by mean-squared reconstruction error and by the average nearest-neighbor distance in latent space as a proxy for extrapolation difficulty. Ablations vary the number of training replicates and the fraction of observed indices, revealing instability with one replicate, saturating gains past a few replicates, and a coverage-versus-quality trade-off governed by neighbor distance.
