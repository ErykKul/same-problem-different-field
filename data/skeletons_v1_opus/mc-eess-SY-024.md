MECHANISM: A continuous latent state on a constrained manifold, together with auxiliary rate and bias quantities, is tracked recursively by a multiplicative error-state estimator. Between observations the nominal state is advanced by numerically integrating nonlinear kinematic and rigid-body dynamics, while a small error-state covariance is propagated through a linearized transition built from the system Jacobian plus an additive process term. Each observation is formed by combining two reference directions with their noisy measured counterparts into orthonormal triads whose product yields a measured orientation; the residual between measured and predicted orientation is mapped to a minimal three-vector, an innovation covariance and gain are computed from the prior covariance and measurement noise, the error state and covariance are updated, the correction is applied multiplicatively, and the error is reset. Wrapped around this is a bank of competing models, each pinned to one hypothesis of a fixed offset parameter on a discrete grid; each model's posterior weight is updated multiplicatively by a Gaussian residual likelihood and renormalized, giving a posterior over the grid. Low-weight hypotheses are pruned, and when a weight-concentration diversity index falls below a threshold the grid is recentered on the weighted-mean hypothesis and refined at finer spacing. The per-model orientations are finally fused by the leading eigenvector of their weighted outer-product matrix. Errors are validated against covariance bounds over many randomized trials.
DOMAIN: spacecraft attitude determination and sensor calibration
STRUCTURE: dense linear algebra
DATA_OBJECT: sequence or time-series
INFERENCE: bayesian posterior
PROBLEM_FORM: estimation
DISTRIBUTION: continuous; gaussian
COMPLEXITY: polynomial iterative
