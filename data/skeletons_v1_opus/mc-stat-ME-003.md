MECHANISM: The aim is to estimate the dependence between two variables, and more generally regression coefficients, using only the ordering of observations, with exact unbiasedness even when values are tied. For each variable, an antisymmetric comparison matrix records the sign of every pairwise ordering across all pairs of observations. Each comparison matrix is double-centered by subtracting row, column, and grand means and zeroing the diagonal, then collapsed into a per-observation vector by summing over one index, an embedding that places the rank information into an inner-product space. A dependence estimate is formed from the standardized inner product of the two embedded vectors, and higher central moments of the embeddings are also computed. A criterion function in the spirit of a likelihood is built from these moments, and it is argued that the bounded, lighter-than-Gaussian-tailed embedded variables make a fixed finite number of moments sufficient. The estimator is shown to be a moment-based point estimate whose score equations are linear in the rank statistics, yielding exact finite-sample unbiasedness, with the variance attaining the relevant information lower bound and asymptotic normality following from the central limit theorem. The framework is extended to a linear model in the embedded response, where the resulting estimator coincides with the ordinary least-squares form and is proven minimum-variance among linear unbiased rank-measurable estimators, with the identity link shown to be the unique admissible monotone link.
DOMAIN: nonparametric and rank-based statistical inference
STRUCTURE: N-body or all-pairs
