MECHANISM: A dynamical system is described by a small set of coupled nonlinear ordinary differential equations governing the evolution of latent state quantities through time, together with unknown static parameters. Uncertainty in the initial state and parameters is represented by an ensemble of sample points drawn from a prior distribution over the joint state-parameter space. Each member is advanced forward in time by numerically integrating the governing equations up to the next moment at which an external measurement becomes available. At each such moment the procedure performs a recursive correction: the ensemble's sample mean and covariance summarize the forecast distribution, a gain is formed from the forecast covariance and the measurement-noise variance, and every member is nudged toward the new observation in proportion to that gain. This update step is the sample-based realization of conditioning a Gaussian prior on a noisy linear observation, producing an analysis ensemble that becomes the prior for the next forecast cycle. The alternation of integrate-forward and condition-on-data steps assimilates each successive measurement and progressively constrains both the latent states and the static parameters. After assimilation the constrained ensemble is integrated further ahead, with no further corrections, to generate probabilistic predictions of future trajectory features. The spread across ensemble members at the prediction horizon quantifies confidence. The scheme is evaluated retrospectively by comparing predicted trajectory landmarks against later observed outcomes.
