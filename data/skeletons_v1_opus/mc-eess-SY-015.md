MECHANISM: An agent learns a mapping from states to a distribution over actions by maximizing expected cumulative discounted reward, augmented with a population of candidate solutions that search the action space in parallel. Several parallel learners each maintain a parametric stochastic action policy and a value estimate sharing a common feature backbone, and each also plays the role of a moving particle holding a position and velocity vector in action space. At each interaction step a learner forms two action proposals, one drawn from its policy and one read from its particle position, and emits a convex blend of the two for continuous actions or a probabilistic switch for discrete ones. An intrinsic bonus equal to a saturating function of the minimum distance from the chosen action to all other particles is added to the environmental reward to reward behavioral spread. Each learner updates its policy by a clipped importance-ratio surrogate on its own trajectory using exponentially weighted advantage estimates, with added value-fit and entropy terms, by stochastic gradient steps. Particle velocities are updated by inertia plus attraction toward each particle's best and the globally best position, with the inertia and the cognitive and social pull coefficients rescaled according to the variance of fitness across the population; high dispersion pushes toward the global attractor and low dispersion toward self-refinement. Periodically the parameters of the best-performing learner are copied to all learners to synchronize exploitation, after which renewed particle mixing regenerates diversity. The cycle of mixed-action sampling, bonus-augmented reward collection, gradient updates, particle updates, broadcasting, and variance-driven rescaling repeats each iteration.
