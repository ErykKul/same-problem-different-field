MECHANISM: This is a descriptive software-tool paper that packages existing network-analysis procedures into a library and demonstrates them on one topical query, contributing no new computational method of its own. It retrieves records and their reference links from an external catalogue, assembles a directed citation graph and an undirected co-authorship graph from a seed set optionally expanded by neighboring nodes, then applies off-the-shelf centrality measures and community-detection routines and visualizes the result. Because every analytical step reuses standard published algorithms, there is no novel estimator, model, or derivation introduced.
