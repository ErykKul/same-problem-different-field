MECHANISM: This work presents a web-based system for interactively viewing collections of multivariate time-series records, not a new computational method of its own. It describes a client-server architecture in which a typed backend service exposes an interface, a document store holds records as structured objects, and a browser front end renders interactive plots, with components packaged as containers. Records are produced offline by previously published approximation and post-processing routines and then ingested through the service interface into internal data structures. The system supports search, per-record browsing, and side-by-side display of records before and after correction. No estimator, model, or algorithm is derived or analyzed here; the referenced predictive and correction procedures live in other works.
