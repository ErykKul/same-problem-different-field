MECHANISM: A pipeline maps multi-source observations of an entity into a sequence of discrete actions through staged learned modules. From an observed value-and-magnitude time series a battery of rolling-window descriptors (moving averages, oscillators, band positions, ratios) is computed deterministically, and an adaptive significance threshold is formed as the recent return standard deviation scaled by a regime-dependent multiplier and clipped to empirical quantiles. This single-step threshold is extended to multi-step horizons by a square-root-of-horizon scaling rule, and the cumulative future return over each horizon is compared against it to assign a three-way ordinal label (up, down, flat). A sequence model is adapted in two stages: supervised next-token cross-entropy minimization to imitate a teacher's structured reasoning-plus-output, then a group-normalized policy-gradient stage maximizing a scalar reward that is a convex combination of horizon-weighted classification correctness and an output-format-validity score. At inference, several analyzer modules each emit structured summaries, a fusion module outputs per-horizon directional probability vectors with confidences, and a decision module maps those plus constraint inputs to a discrete position-change action via argmax over the probability vectors. The full loop is evaluated by replaying the action sequence over a held-out period and computing cumulative return, a risk-adjusted return ratio, and a worst peak-to-trough loss, compared against a passive hold and across the two model variants over many independent single-asset accounts.
