MECHANISM: A system of unit-length vector variables sits on the sites of a regular two-dimensional lattice, with an energy functional summing pairwise aligning and antisymmetric cross-product couplings over neighboring sites plus a linear external-field term. The pairwise coupling strengths are made explicit functions of inter-site distance through a parametrized radial form with a cutoff, so that a uniaxial deformation of the lattice geometry makes the neighbor distances inequivalent and renders the couplings anisotropic. Equilibrium configurations are sampled by two complementary stochastic schemes: a coupled set of damped stochastic differential equations advancing both positions/momenta and the precessing vector variables under an effective field plus Gaussian noise, and an accept-reject single-site update scheme augmented with energy-conserving rotational moves under a gradual temperature-lowering schedule. From the sampled configurations one computes the energy-variance-based specific heat, scalar triple-product chirality order parameters over elementary and enlarged plaquettes, and a static structure factor as the squared magnitude of the spatial Fourier sum of a transverse component, averaged over samples. Sweeping the deformation magnitude, temperature, and field, the resulting order-parameter values and reciprocal-space patterns are used to delineate boundaries between distinct ordered and disordered regimes and to detect a sign change in the chirality signaling a topological-charge switch. A control case with one coupling removed isolates which interaction is responsible for each regime.
