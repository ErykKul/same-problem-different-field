MECHANISM: The object is a backward equation for a continuous-time process tied to a fixed terminal value, whose increment is prescribed by a generator function of the current state and its noise-coefficient components, plus a finite-variation forcing term, minus martingale increments driven by a continuous Gaussian-type noise and several point-process jump martingales each with its own predictable intensity. A martingale representation result guarantees that any square-integrable martingale in the enlarged information flow decomposes into a continuous stochastic integral plus jump stochastic integrals, which fixes the solution components. A priori energy estimates are derived by applying the change-of-variables formula to an exponentially weighted square of the difference of two solutions, conditioning, and bounding cross terms with the generator's Lipschitz constant via elementary quadratic inequalities. These estimates make a candidate map (which sends a guessed process to the solution of the equation with that guess frozen in the generator) a strict contraction on a weighted complete space, so a fixed-point argument yields existence and uniqueness. Ordering of the solution components under generator inequalities gives comparison and strict-comparison results. For a generator affine in all arguments, an adjoint forward exponential process is built as the stochastic exponential of the affine coefficients; multiplying it by the solution and applying the product rule cancels the drift, so the leading component admits a closed-form conditional-expectation representation against the terminal value and the forcing term, with the form differing depending on whether the forcing is predictable or merely optional. The construction is applied to value and replicate a terminal-payoff contract in linear and nonlinear complete settings.
