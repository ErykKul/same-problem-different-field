MECHANISM: The work describes the design of a shared repository that aggregates references to externally hosted resources and organizes them into a small number of thematic catalogs. Each item is given a persistent identifier and a record conforming to a common descriptor schema, with fields extracted from source documentation and then aligned by manual inspection. Incoming records pass through a pipeline that checks field completeness, format validity, and usage permissions, detects duplicates by matching identifying fields, and then publishes approved entries. No mathematical or algorithmic method is introduced; retrieval is by attribute filtering and keyword matching over the descriptors. The contribution is an organizational and governance framework rather than a computation.
