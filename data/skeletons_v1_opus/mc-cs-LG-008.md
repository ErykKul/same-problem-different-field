MECHANISM: A binary label indicating future occurrence of an event is predicted for each entity from a fixed-length vector of mixed categorical and numeric attributes summarized over a historical window. Records are first assembled into case and control groups, with controls matched to cases by the timing of a reference point, and attributes aggregated over an observation window that is separated from the prediction point by a gap to avoid leakage. Categorical fields are collapsed into coarse groups and one-hot encoded, presence indicators are made binary, and numeric fields are averaged, while missing numeric entries are deliberately left unfilled so the learner can route them. The model is an additive ensemble of regression trees fit by stagewise minimization of a regularized classification loss, where each successive tree fits the gradient of the loss and split points are chosen greedily; the regularization penalizes tree complexity and the magnitude of leaf scores. Variable selection is done first by fitting a preliminary ensemble and ranking attributes by their contribution to splits, then keeping a top subset. The continuous structural parameters governing depth, number of trees, step size, subsampling rates, and penalty strengths are tuned by a sequential model-based search that maximizes a held-out ranking score on an internal validation partition. The final ensemble is refit on the full training partition with the chosen parameters and evaluated on a disjoint test partition. Performance is measured by threshold-free ranking summaries and threshold-based agreement counts, and compared against two fixed linear index formulas computed only where their required inputs are present.
