MECHANISM: A multivariate sequence of observations is modeled as generated by a hidden discrete state that follows a first-order Markov chain, where within each state the observations are drawn from a heavy-tailed elliptical density parameterized by a location, a scale matrix, and a tail parameter. The transition matrix and the per-state emission parameters are fit by an expectation-maximization procedure that alternately computes posterior responsibilities of states and reestimates parameters to increase the likelihood, and the number of states is chosen by a penalized-likelihood criterion. Each time point is then assigned its most probable state, partitioning the sequence into per-state subsamples. Within each subsample, for every ordered pair of component series a nested-regression test evaluates whether including lagged values of one series significantly improves the one-step prediction of the other beyond its own lagged history, yielding a test statistic and significance level, with a multiplicity correction across all directed pairs and a selection of lag order by the same penalized criterion. Observations whose required lags cross a state boundary are excluded. The discovered directional, state-specific predictive links are then checked for stability across subperiods and validated against independent labeled episodes with a binomial tail probability, and a naive thresholded rule probes whether the predictive link yields economic value.
DOMAIN: empirical finance, equity factor return dynamics
STRUCTURE: graphical models
DATA_OBJECT: sequence or time-series
INFERENCE: maximum likelihood
PROBLEM_FORM: decision or test
DISTRIBUTION: heavy-tailed; other: student-t
