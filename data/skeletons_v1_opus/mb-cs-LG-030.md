MECHANISM: Given a partially-oriented relational structure over a set of discrete entities, the goal is to resolve every ambiguous pairwise direction into a fully oriented acyclic structure. Each discrete entity with several levels is expanded into a one-hot indicator vector, and the full system state is the concatenation of these indicators. A real weight matrix over the expanded coordinates is partitioned into blocks, one block per ordered pair of entities, so each block parameterizes level-to-level influence. A binary feasibility mask, derived from the input structure, zeroes out forbidden connections; the effective adjacency is an elementwise nonlinearity of the weights times the mask. Predicted level probabilities for each entity are formed by a per-entity softmax over masked linear scores, and the primary objective is the cross-entropy between observed one-hot levels and these predictions. Three penalties are added: a grouped sparsity norm that shrinks whole blocks, a product term penalizing both directions of a pair being simultaneously active, and a term preventing supported pairs from being fully pruned. The weighted sum is minimized by gradient-based optimization, with an asymmetric initialization on ambiguous pairs to break ties. After optimization, a pairwise direction strength is aggregated per block and thresholded to extract edges, and any remaining directed cycles are removed greedily by cutting the weakest edge until acyclicity holds. Structural recovery is evaluated against ground-truth structures by edit distance and overlap scores across a range of system sizes.
DOMAIN: causal structure discovery, machine learning
STRUCTURE: numerical optimization
