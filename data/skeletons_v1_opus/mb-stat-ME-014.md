MECHANISM: A non-negative data matrix is approximated as a product of a shared non-negative basis matrix and a score matrix, where the scores are parameterized as a non-negative coefficient matrix times a fixed design matrix of covariates plus unit-specific deviation terms. Estimation minimizes a penalized squared-error criterion with a quadratic penalty on the deviation terms, subject to non-negativity and a column-normalization constraint, by alternating three block updates. The deviation block has a closed-form ridge-type solution per unit using a shared small inverse; the basis and coefficient blocks use elementwise multiplicative non-negative updates with positive-part stabilization. The effective complexity contributed by the deviation block is monitored through the trace of a regularized projection operator and capped by a one-dimensional search on the penalty strength. For inference on the coefficient matrix, the deviation block is profiled out conditionally on the estimated basis, yielding per-unit residuals and score contributions via an envelope argument. A curvature matrix with a tensor-product structure is formed, reduced to account for the profiling, and combined with an outer-product-of-scores matrix into a sandwich variance estimator. Bootstrap replicates are produced by a one-step linear update around the estimate driven by randomly reweighted score contributions, then projected onto the non-negativity constraint, avoiding repeated constrained re-optimization. Finite-sample behavior, coverage, and boundary effects are assessed by repeated synthetic-data trials. The whole procedure couples a constrained matrix decomposition with resampling-based uncertainty quantification.
