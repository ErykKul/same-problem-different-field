# A Novel Multiple Imputation Approach For Parameter Estimation in Observation-Driven Time Series Models With Missing Data

## Abstract

In this paper we propose a novel multiple imputation method specifically designed for parameter estimation in observation-driven models (ODM). The approach takes advantage of the iterative nature of the systematic component in ODM to propagate the dependence structure through missing data, thereby minimizing its impact on estimation. Unlike traditional imputation techniques, the proposed method accommodates continuous, discrete, and mixed-type data while preserving key distributional and dependence properties. Under general conditions, we prove that the proposed method converges almost surely to the true parameter, regardless of the missing mechanism or the proportion of missing data. We evaluate its performance through Monte Carlo simulations in the context of GARMA models, considering time series with up to 70% missing data. Two practical applications to environmelntal time series further demonstrates its practical utility. Keywords: time series analysis; observation-driven models; non-gaussian time series; missing data; multiple imputation; convergence of algorithms. MSC: 62M10, 62F12, 62E20, 62G20, 60G15.
