## Abstract

Popov Institut für Theoretische Physik, Leibniz Universität Hannover Appelstraße 2, 30167 Hannover, Germany Email: alexander.popov@itp.uni-hannover.de Abstract We consider the nature of the wave function using the example of a harmonic oscillator. We show that the eigenfunctions $\psi\_{n}{=}z^{n}$ of the quantum Hamiltonian in the complex Bargmann-Fock-Segal representation with $z\in\mathbb{C}$ are the coordinates of a classical oscillator with energy $E\_{n}=\hbar\omega n$ , $n=0,1,2,...\,$ . They are defined on conical spaces ${\mathbb{C}}/{\mathbb{Z}}\_{n}$ with cone angles $2\pi/n$ , which are embedded as subspaces in the phase space $\mathbb{C}$ of the classical oscillator. Here ${\mathbb{Z}}\_{n}$ is the finite cyclic group of rotations of the space $\mathbb{C}$ by an angle $2\pi/n$ .
