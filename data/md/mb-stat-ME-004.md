However, it lacks a decisiontheoretic foundation for this purpose. To address this, we examine its negatively oriented counterpart, which we refer to as Nash‐Sutcliffe loss, defined as LNS = 1 −NSE. We prove that LNS is strictly consistent for an elicitable and identifiable multi-dimensional functional, which we name the Nash-Sutcliffe functional.
