Torres Alegre ORCID: 0000-0002-6798-8776 Pontifical Catholic University of Chile, Santiago, Chile onill@uc.cl Abstract Efficient resolution of neuroinflammation and debris clearance are key determinants of successful central nervous system (CNS) regeneration. Regenerative vertebrates such as Danio rerio often show faster immune resolution and debris clearance than mammals, yet the molecular determinants underlying these differences remain incompletely understood. TAM receptor tyrosine kinases (Tyro3, Axl, and Mertk) and their ligands Gas6 and Protein S are central regulators of phagocytosis and immune resolution in the nervous system, but whether intrinsic structural properties of these receptor–ligand complexes contribute to regenerative efficiency has not been systematically explored.
