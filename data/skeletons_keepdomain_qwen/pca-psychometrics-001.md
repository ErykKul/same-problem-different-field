It highlights the risks of assumption violations and the need for careful data screening in factor analysis, particularly when analyzing item-level data from questionnaires. The discussion includes comparisons between traditional and alternative approaches, such as the use of polychoric correlations in non-linear factor analysis. The paper concludes with recommendations for conducting analyses that are robust to assumption violations and sensitive to data quality issues.
