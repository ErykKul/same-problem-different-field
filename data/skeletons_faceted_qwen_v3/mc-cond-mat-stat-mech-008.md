MECHANISM: This paper computes spin-operator matrix elements in the fermionic basis of the critical Ising chain. The process begins by analyzing matrices formed from Toeplitz symbols, leveraging their near-Cauchy properties to derive square-root product expressions for leg functions. These products are converted into integral representations, which simplify to Binet’s second integral and its Hermite generalization in the finite volume scaling limit. This leads to explicit product formulas for spin-operator form factors in the scaling limit, expressed as rational numbers up to √2. The normalization factor of the spin-operator is determined through ground state overlap calculations, involving the coefficient G(1/2)G(3/2). A Fredholm determinant identity is derived by expanding the spin-spin two-point correlator in the fermionic basis, enabling the convergence of the rescaled correlator to the CFT version on a cylinder. The computation involves algebraic manipulations of gamma matrices, eigenvalue decompositions, and transformations between fermionic creation/annihilation operators and spin operators, with careful handling of branch cuts and determinant signs to ensure consistency in the critical model.
