MECHANISM: The paper computes two Markov chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) algorithms for sampling from a discrete Gaussian distribution over a lattice. The first algorithm, independent MHK, uses a proposal distribution generated by Klein’s algorithm to suggest candidate states, then applies an acceptance-rejection rule based on the ratio of target and proposal densities. The Markov chain induced by this algorithm is shown to be uniformly ergodic, converging exponentially fast to the stationary distribution regardless of initial conditions. The convergence rate is analyzed using theta series derived from the lattice basis, query point, and standard deviation. The second algorithm, symmetric SMH, employs a symmetric proposal distribution between consecutive states, achieving geometric ergodicity where convergence depends on the initial state. Both algorithms leverage the structure of the lattice Gaussian distribution, with the independent MHK algorithm having polynomial space complexity and time complexity dependent on the standard deviation. The analysis involves bounding the total variation distance between the chain’s distribution and the target, using spectral gap properties and coupling techniques to establish ergodicity guarantees.
