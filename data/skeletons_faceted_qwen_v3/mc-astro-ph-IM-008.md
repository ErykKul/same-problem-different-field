MECHANISM: This paper describes the design, performance evaluation, and technical improvements of the INFUSE instrument, a far ultraviolet integral field spectrograph. It details the optical configuration, including an f/16 Cassegrain telescope, image slicer, holographic gratings, and a microchannel plate detector. The paper discusses challenges in vacuum sealing, mirror coatings, grating alignment, and scattered light mitigation. It outlines the first flight's results, including successful detection of ionizing gas in the Cygnus Loop, and plans for the second flight, such as improved vacuum performance, xenon-enhanced lithium fluoride coatings, and adjustments to the aspect camera and fold mirror. The paper also introduces SUMO, a secondary payload testing digital micromirror devices for multi-object spectroscopy. It emphasizes hardware engineering, observational capabilities, and flight-specific adaptations rather than computational algorithms or mathematical models.
