MECHANISM: This paper proposes a hybrid framework integrating human-led systematic review with GenAI-assisted pre-screening to address scalability and reproducibility challenges in literature synthesis. The method involves (1) defining a search intent statement, (2) calculating semantic similarity scores between records and the intent using a mixture model distribution, (3) plotting the score histogram to identify decision boundaries between high/low relevance groups, (4) applying statistical thresholds to filter records, and (5) using GenAI to summarize low-relevance records while retaining human oversight for high-relevance cases. The framework emphasizes deterministic statistical pre-screening to ensure reproducibility, with human validation of all outcomes. No novel mathematical algorithm or computational model is presented; the focus is on workflow integration and methodological guidelines rather than algorithmic innovation. The paper does not describe any new estimator, simulation, or proof, but rather outlines a structured approach to augment existing PRISMA protocols with AI tools. The statistical mixture model for similarity scores is standard and not algorithmically novel. The framework's core contribution lies in its application methodology rather than computational innovation.
