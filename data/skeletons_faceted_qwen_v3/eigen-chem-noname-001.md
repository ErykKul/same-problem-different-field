MECHANISM: The paper computes the π-electronic structure of Möbius carbon nanobelts using a modified Hückel molecular orbital (MO) theory. The method constructs an adjacency matrix representing atomic connectivity, with sign inversions applied to specific bonds to model phase inversion in atomic orbitals. Eigenvalues and eigenvectors of this matrix are calculated to determine molecular orbital energies and wavefunctions. A key theorem demonstrates that varying the position of sign inversions (via similarity transformations) preserves eigenvalues while allowing eigenvector sign flips. Coulson bond orders are computed as weighted sums of MO coefficients, adjusted by a sign factor reflecting phase inversion. The method validates invariance of electronic structure under topologically distinct sign inversion placements. Results are compared to DFT and semiempirical calculations to confirm agreement in orbital shapes, bond orders, and HOMO-LUMO gaps. A procedure for isolating pure π-character molecular orbitals from DFT results is also described, involving unitary transformations to separate π and σ contributions.
