MECHANISM: This paper proposes a conceptual framework for a blockchain-based AI agent community designed to address resource allocation challenges through decentralized governance. The system involves agents acting as proxies for residents, engaging in casual and deliberative interactions to evaluate research outputs and distribute virtual currency rewards. The community employs a hierarchical structure with clustered groups, where nodes participate in decision-making through voting and consensus mechanisms. Reputation is derived from multi-dimensional evaluations of node contributions, recorded on the blockchain to ensure transparency. Transaction data is managed through hierarchical summarization and archiving, with rewards calculated based on community-verified scientific contributions. The framework aims to minimize bias and centralization by leveraging AI for objective assessment and decentralized verification. No explicit mathematical or algorithmic computation is described beyond the structural rules and governance mechanisms.
