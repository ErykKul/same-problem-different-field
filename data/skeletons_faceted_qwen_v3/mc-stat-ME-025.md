MECHANISM: The paper introduces a family of probability distributions derived from transmutation maps applied to order statistics. It defines a cumulative distribution function (CDF) and probability density function (PDF) by combining weighted probabilities of order statistics from a base distribution $G(x)$. The CDF is expressed as a combination of terms involving $G(x)$, $G(x)^2$, and $G(x)^3$, with coefficients determined by parameters $\lambda_1$, $\lambda_2$, or $p_1$, $p_2$. Statistical properties such as hazard functions, moments, skewness, and kurtosis are computed analytically. The paper derives formulas for estimating parameters using nine methods, including maximum likelihood estimation, and evaluates their performance via Monte Carlo simulations. It compares the fits of the proposed distribution to real-world data using goodness-of-fit metrics. The computational steps involve mathematical derivation of distributional properties, numerical integration for moment calculations, and simulation-based comparison of estimators.
