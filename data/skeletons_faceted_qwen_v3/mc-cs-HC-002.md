MECHANISM: The paper investigates the effects of limited field of view (FOV) on musical collaboration using avatars in extended reality (XR) through empirical studies. It describes a within-subjects experiment comparing three conditions: an unrestricted FOV holographic setup (HoloJam), a standard AR setup with limited FOV (52°), and a modified AR setup with a notification system (Mini Musicians). The study measures co-presence, gesture recognition, enjoyment, and reaction time by observing musicians during jam sessions with and without visual occlusion. Data is collected through video recordings, participant interviews, and analysis of body language and communication patterns. The paper concludes that limited FOV reduces musical collaboration effectiveness but that notifications can mitigate some negative effects. No computational model, algorithm, or mathematical method is described; the focus is on empirical observation and user experience analysis.
