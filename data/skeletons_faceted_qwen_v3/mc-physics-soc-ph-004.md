The model highlights how traits form clusters that reinforce each other, creating stable configurations or driving paradigm shifts when inconsistencies accumulate. It draws parallels between cultural systems and scientific paradigms, noting how systemic transformations occur through crises and replacements of frameworks. The paper also examines how modern technologies like LLMs act as cultural embeddings that reshape information flows and future evolution. It concludes that cultural change is driven by interdependencies, path dependence, and the co-evolution of traits within structured systems.
