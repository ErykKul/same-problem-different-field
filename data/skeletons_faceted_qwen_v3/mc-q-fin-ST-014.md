MECHANISM: The paper introduces a multidimensional Log S-fBM model (mLog S-fBM) by extending the univariate Log S-fBM framework. It defines a multidimensional Stationary fractional Brownian motion (mS-fBM) with marginals following S-fBM dynamics and a cross-covariance structure parametrized by a correlation scale T, intermittency parameters (λi), Hurst exponents (Hi), co-intermittency matrix (ξi,j), and co-Hurst matrix (Hi,j). The mLog S-fBM is constructed by exponentiating the mS-fBM to model volatility components, preserving Gaussian dependencies. The model is valid for co-Hurst matrices with entries in [0, 1/2), allowing transitions between rough and multifractal regimes. A small intermittency approximation is generalized to derive a Generalized Method of Moments (GMM) calibration procedure, estimating cross-covariance parameters for pairs of marginals. The method is validated on synthetic data and applied to S&P 500 market data, revealing diagonal Hurst matrix entries close to zero (multifractal behavior) and off-diagonal entries aligning with the S&P 500 index's Hurst exponent (rough volatility).
