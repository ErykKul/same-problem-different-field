MECHANISM: The paper computes an online finetuning framework for sequence-based decision-making models using reinforcement learning gradients. It begins by collecting trajectories through interaction with an environment, where each trajectory consists of a sequence of states, actions, and returns. To enable online adaptation, the method replaces the standard hindsight return relabeling with a sub-trajectory-based optimization approach. Sub-trajectories are generated by resetting the environment at selected points within full trajectories, allowing for localized credit assignment. For each sub-trajectory, the algorithm computes a sequence-level importance ratio by comparing the probability of the sub-trajectory under the current policy to that under a reference policy. This ratio is used to weight the advantage estimates derived from the sub-trajectory's cumulative reward. The advantage is normalized by subtracting the mean and dividing by the standard deviation of rewards across a group of sub-trajectories. The policy is then updated by maximizing a modified objective function that incorporates these weighted advantages, while also applying a KL divergence penalty to prevent excessive deviation from the reference policy. To enhance exploration, the method actively selects sub-trajectories based on the variance of the policy's action distribution. Additionally, when environment resetting is not feasible, an auxiliary Q-function is trained to evaluate candidate actions under shared states. These components collectively enable pure reinforcement learning-based finetuning of pretrained models without reliance on supervised objectives.
