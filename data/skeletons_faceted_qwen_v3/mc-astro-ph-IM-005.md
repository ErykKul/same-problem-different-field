Calibration campaigns collect photon counts across energy ranges to meet precision requirements, and statistical uncertainty in modulation is quantified as a function of detected counts. The analysis involves deterministic corrections based on physical models, including charge accumulation in GEMs and absorption of dimethyl ether by adhesive components.
