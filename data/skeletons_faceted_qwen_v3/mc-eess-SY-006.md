MECHANISM: This paper introduces a benchmark framework for evaluating large language models as autonomous agents in UAV missions under 6G network conditions. The framework models UAV operations as multi-turn conversational control loops between an LLM-based agent and a human operator, with decisions constrained by schema validity, mission policies, and safety bounds. It integrates dual-action layers for tool calls and agent coordination, evaluates 17 LLMs on a corpus of 113k AI-generated conversational episodes, and proposes a composite metric combining task outcome, safety compliance, tool consistency, interaction quality, network robustness, and communication cost. The benchmark measures performance under varying network conditions (latency, packet loss, throughput) and normalizes scores by reliability and efficiency. It does not introduce a novel algorithm, model, or computational method but establishes a structured evaluation protocol for existing LLMs in safety-critical, network-constrained environments.
