MECHANISM: The paper computes a bibliometric analysis of research trends and collaborations by retrieving and processing a dataset of publications from the Web of Science. It extracts metadata such as authors, institutions, keywords, citations, and publication years, then applies clustering algorithms to identify research hotspots, trends, and collaborative networks. Citation metrics (e.g., total local and global citation scores) are calculated to assess the impact of publications and institutions. Temporal analysis is performed to track the growth of research output over time, and visualization tools map institutional and country-level collaborations. The method does not involve statistical modeling or inference but focuses on descriptive analysis of publication patterns, keyword co-occurrence, and network structures derived from citation and co-authorship data.
