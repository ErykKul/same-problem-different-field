MECHANISM: The paper computes the well-posedness and numerical approximation of a time-fractional partial differential equation (PDE) with a Riemann–Liouville fractional derivative acting on the diffusion term. The equation is derived by convolving a classical Fisher–KPP equation with a Sonine kernel, leading to an equivalent formulation involving a Caputo derivative. The analysis involves proving local and global existence of weak solutions using a Galerkin approximation, a priori estimates via a Bihari–Henry–Gronwall inequality, and handling nonlinear terms through Hölder, Young, and Sobolev inequalities. A nonuniform convolution quadrature scheme is developed for numerical approximation, and the paper compares the dynamics of the physically consistent model with those of a conventional Caputo formulation. The computational steps include defining fractional Sobolev–Bochner spaces, establishing embeddings, and applying integral inequalities to derive bounds on solutions. The numerical experiments validate the theoretical results by simulating the qualitative differences between models.
